The method

How The Chicago Hood & Duct Ledger is ranked

Short version: we look for businesses that actually serve Chicago, say what they do on their own site, and can be reached. Nobody buys a place on the list.

The one disclosure

No. 01, Baffle Works, is owned by King Holdings Ventures LLC, the same company that publishes this guide. It is labelled on its card, held to the same criteria, and nobody else pays for a place.

01 Places actually named

The neighborhoods and towns named on the company's own page, not a claimed radius. Within each group, more places named ranks higher; a company that names no towns ranks after those that do. A county counts for nothing here, so FilterShine, which names counties only, sits in the second group.

02 A number that reaches someone

Every entry prints a phone number. Ties go to the company that prints hours, 24/7 or after-hours service, then to one that says which cleaning standard it follows.

03 What they publish

Standards, certifications, years in business and prices are shown only as printed on the company's page and labelled as its own claim. None of the twelve ranked companies prints a price.

04 No pay-to-play

Position is not for sale. The featured partner, Baffle Works, is ours and is labelled. Everything after it is ordered on the criteria above.

What a "featured partner" means

Slot No. 01 is a partner of this guide and is labelled as such. It is not a paid ranking for the other slots, and the partner is one business in the same trade, held to the same criteria.

The featured business is owned by King Holdings Ventures LLC, the same company that publishes this guide. That is why the list can stay free and uncluttered. It is also why the label is on the card and on this page rather than buried.

What is printed, and what is not

  • Name, town, phone and website come from the business's own site or its public listing.
  • No star ratings or review counts are printed in this edition: the order comes from what each company publishes on its own site, not from review sites.
  • No invented review quotes, no "since 1998", no crew names unless the business publishes them.
  • Prices are only printed when the business publishes them.

Corrections

Wrong number, closed down, moved town, or you own the business and want the entry fixed? Send a correction and we will change it, including removing an entry on request.

Who publishes this

The Chicago Hood & Duct Ledger is compiled by KING HOLDINGS VENTURES LLC in the Chicago area, and written by hand. It is a guide, not an agency for the businesses listed.
