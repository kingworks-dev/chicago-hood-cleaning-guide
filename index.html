    The kitchen year

    What to do, and when

    Most grease trouble is the quiet kind: a filter nobody changed, a duct nobody opened, a fan that was never looked at.

    Any month

    Look at the filters

    Pull a filter and look at it. If the mesh is dark and dripping, you are overdue, whatever the calendar says.

    Before the holidays

    Book ahead of the rush

    Heavy fryer and grill volume means more grease. Book a cleaning before the December parties, not after.

    Busy season

    Ask about your cycle

    How often you need cleaning depends on how you cook. Ask any company to set the cycle for your volume and fuel.

    Winter

    Roof access

    Ice and snow can delay rooftop fan work. Ask whether the roof is part of the visit before the first hard freeze.

    The date that matters: the one on your service sticker.

    The short answers

    Ask us something else
    01How much does restaurant hood cleaning cost in Chicago?

    None of the twelve ranked companies prints a price; most offer a free quote or say cost depends on the system. Baffle Works, our partner, prints $375 per cleaning for a single hood and $550 per cleaning for a standard kitchen. Ask any company for a written price that names the hood, duct and fan.

    02Who answers at night?

    By their own pages, Chicago Hood Cleaning Services says 24 hours a day, 7 days a week and Sta-Kleen prints 24/7. Chicago Hood Cleaning Pros offers after-hours and overnight scheduling when available, and Airways Systems offers scheduled or emergency cleanings. Clean Wash Remove answers Monday to Saturday, 8 to 5.

    03Should I ask about NFPA 96 and IKECA?

    Yes. Chicago Hood Cleaning Pros, Airways, Chicago Hood Cleaning Services, Kitchen Guard and Olympia say they follow or are certified to NFPA 96; Kitchen Guard, Olympia, Airways and Phoenix cite IKECA. Each is the company's own claim, so ask for the paperwork before a job.

    04Hood only, or the whole duct?

    Ask what is in the price. Several pages name hoods, filters, ducts and fans together; FilterShine focuses on filter exchange, and Clean Wash Remove covers accessible exhaust surfaces. A service sticker should show the date and the next due date.

    05What if my neighborhood is not listed?

    Call anyway. Several companies serve beyond the places they name, and the filters above only show what each page prints.

    No. The first entry is a partner of ours and is labelled. The rest are ordered on the public method, and no company paid for its place.
